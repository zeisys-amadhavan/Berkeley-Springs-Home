# Exterior Painting and Finish Design Decisions

This file records the exterior color, painting, and closely related finish decisions made for the Berkeley Springs home. It is intended to preserve the design history and distinguish settled decisions from items that are still preferences.

## Current / settled palette

### Siding
- **Color:** BEHR Very Navy
- **Code:** M500-7
- **Sheen:** Satin
- This is the selected primary exterior siding color.

### Trim, fascia, and soffit
- **Color:** BEHR Ultra Pure White
- **Sheen:** Semi-gloss
- Ultra Pure White was selected over warmer/off-white alternatives.

### Railings
- **Color:** Navy, coordinated with the Very Navy siding.
- The exterior simulations use navy railings.

### Deck
- **Finish direction:** Warm cedar-brown / natural warm wood tone.
- The deck is intended to remain visually warm so it contrasts with the cool navy-and-white house palette.
- Later simulations show the deck in a cedar/natural walnut-brown family.

### Exterior fixtures
- **Color:** Matte black / black.
- Exterior light fixtures in the approved simulations are black.

### Primer
- **Product direction:** BEHR exterior primer was selected for the painted exterior work.

## Stone / tile veneer coordination

- Approximately **25% of the exterior wall area** has been reserved for tile/stone veneer rather than paint.
- The leading veneer is **MSI Silver Travertine ledger panel**.
- The Silver Travertine is intended to bridge the navy siding, white trim, and gray metal-roof palette.
- The simulations showing Silver Travertine are stored under `simulations/exterior/`.

## Roof color direction

The metal roof is not a paint decision, but it is part of the coordinated exterior palette and is recorded here because it materially affects the color scheme.

### Leading choice
- **Charcoal Gray** is the current preferred roof color.

### Close alternative
- **Matte Black** remains appealing, but Charcoal Gray is preferred because it is less visually heavy against the very dark navy siding and coordinates better with the Silver Travertine.

### Lower-ranked alternatives
- Weathered Zinc
- Dark Bronze
- Medium Bronze

The roof comparison simulation is stored as:
- `simulations/exterior/roof-color-comparison.png`

## White selection history

Several light trim colors were considered. **Ultra Pure White** was ultimately preferred because it provides the cleanest contrast against Very Navy.

Alternatives considered and not selected:
- Polar Bear
- Frost
- Whipped Cream

## Foundation / lower-wall design history

Earlier in the design process, the exposed concrete/foundation was evaluated in gray, greige, charcoal, stone-look, and white treatments.

- A pure **Ultra Pure White masonry finish** was the preferred painted-foundation solution when evaluating paint-only approaches.
- The design later evolved toward using **MSI Silver Travertine ledger stone** over substantial lower-wall areas.
- Therefore, where veneer is installed, the Silver Travertine supersedes the earlier painted-foundation concept.

## Doors and windows

The design simulations often show white doors/window surrounds as part of the overall navy-and-white composition.

Implementation status from the completed painting work:
- Exterior **trim was painted**.
- **Doors and windows themselves were not painted** in the completed work referenced during the October 2026 review.

This distinction should be preserved when estimating remaining labor.

## Visual reference set

The exterior simulations in `simulations/exterior/` document the intended coordinated palette:
- Very Navy siding
- Ultra Pure White trim and soffit
- MSI Silver Travertine ledger stone
- Warm cedar-toned decking
- Navy railings
- Black exterior fixtures
- Charcoal Gray metal roof as the leading roof choice

These simulation files should be used as the visual reference when discussing future painting, roofing, veneer, decking, railing, or landscaping work.

## Design intent

The overall exterior is intended to read as:
- dark navy wooded-retreat architecture,
- crisp white architectural trim,
- warm natural wood at decks,
- cool light stone at the lower walls,
- dark understated roof and fixtures.

The intent is to look polished and cohesive while still fitting a wooded Berkeley Springs setting rather than appearing overly suburban.
