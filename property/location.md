## Accuracy note

These coordinates should **not** be treated as foot-level survey coordinates.

The iPhone Compass display shown in the screenshots reports whole arc-seconds only. At this latitude, one arc-second represents roughly:

- about 101 ft north/south
- about 78 ft east/west

Therefore, even though the physical measurement point was about one foot from the deck-side wall, the displayed latitude/longitude is only suitable as a **property/site reference**, not as a precise surveyed point.

For foot-level or sub-foot positioning, use a surveyed control point, RTK GNSS, or another high-accuracy positioning method.
